Privacy Policy

Centre Point Tickets · Hotel Amarjit Pvt Ltd (Centre Point, Nagpur) · Last updated 31 October 2026

Centre Point Tickets is the ticketing system used by Hotel Amarjit Pvt Ltd ("we", "us") to register guests for our events, issue entry tickets, and check guests in at the venue. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Name, mobile number and WhatsApp number
  • Email address, gender and age, if you provide them
  • Ticket details: category, price paid, registration number and QR code
  • Check-in records: when and at which gate your ticket was scanned

How we use it

  • To create your ticket and send it to you, including by WhatsApp
  • To verify your ticket and check you in at the event
  • To keep attendance and sales records for the event

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the services we need to run the ticketing system:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), to deliver your ticket on WhatsApp
  • Our hosting and database providers, which store the data on our behalf

We may also disclose information when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed to run the event and maintain our records, and then delete it.

Deleting your data

To ask us to delete your information, email dm6891493@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and include the mobile number you registered with. We will confirm once your information has been deleted.
